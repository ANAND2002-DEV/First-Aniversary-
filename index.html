05 · A letter for you

Some things
are better written.

So I wrote down what this first year with you means to me.

To My Sneha, ♥

One year.

It feels strange to say it out loud, because somehow it feels like yesterday when you first became a part of my life, and at the same time, it feels like I have known you for much longer.

I still remember the first picture you sent me. I didn't know then how important that little moment would become. And then came our first call on 5 October 2025—a simple call that became the beginning of so many memories, conversations, smiles, misunderstandings, apologies, late-night talks, and countless little moments that I will always keep close to my heart.

We may not have had a perfect year. We have had our moments—times when we understood each other perfectly, and times when we couldn't understand each other at all. We have laughed, missed each other, worried about each other, and sometimes hurt each other too.

But through all of it, there is one thing I know for sure:

I am grateful that it was you.

Thank you for coming into my life and making ordinary days feel a little more special. Thank you for every call, every message, every smile, every little effort, and every moment when you simply stayed.

Distance may have kept us apart sometimes, but it never made you feel far from my heart.

Today, on our first anniversary, I don't want to promise you a perfect future. I want to promise you something more real—

I will keep choosing you.

I will keep learning how to understand you better.
I will try to protect the peace between us.
I will listen when you need to be heard.
I will apologize when I am wrong.
I will celebrate your happiness as if it were my own.
And whenever life becomes difficult, I hope we remember why we started.

I don't know exactly what every tomorrow will look like.

But I know who I want beside me while I find out.

You.

Sneha, you are not just a beautiful chapter of my life. You have become a part of the story I want to keep writing.

From our first picture…
to our first call…
to all the days that followed…

this is only our first year.

And if you are willing, I want there to be many more chapters after this one.

Happy 1st Anniversary, my love. ♥

Thank you for being you.

Forever yours,
Anand ♥
